Common questions

Does the app need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app. It works on a plane, on the road, anywhere.

How do I get new creatures or fixes?

Update the app from the App Store. New data comes with new app versions.

How do I change the language or switch to dark mode?

Tap the gear button on the Home screen to open Settings. Pick a language under "Language", and Light, Dark or Phone setting under "Appearance".

Does the app collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
