Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PokéET. is made for children. We take their privacy seriously, so we keep it simple:

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking.
  • We do not share any data with anyone, because we have none.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings (language and light/dark choice) and its creature data on your device only. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 6 and up. Because no personal information is collected, no parental consent for data collection is needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
